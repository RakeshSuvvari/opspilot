# INC-2025-004 — Payment Startup Failure After Configuration Change

## Impact
The payment service had zero available replicas after a deployment configuration change. Requests depending on payment failed until configuration was restored.

## Timeline
A new payment Deployment revision was applied. New pods immediately restarted and never reached readiness. Application logs reported that `DATABASE_URL` was not configured.

## Root cause
A deployment manifest update unintentionally omitted the environment reference used to provide `DATABASE_URL`.

## Resolution
The configuration reference was restored and a new rollout completed successfully.

## Prevention
Validate required environment references during CI and keep operational secrets outside application manifests.
